Privacy

This site uses GoatCounter to measure anonymous, aggregate traffic and which blog posts are viewed. GoatCounter does not use cookies or persistent identifiers.

The analytics data may include the page visited, the referring website, browser type, screen size, and approximate geographic region. GoatCounter stores aggregate statistics rather than individual page-view records. The data is used only to understand site usage and is not sold or used for advertising.

Analytics reports are private and accessible only to the site owner through GoatCounter's authenticated dashboard. For more information, see GoatCounter's privacy policy and its GDPR information.
